OUR STORY

Started with one oven and a lot of stubbornness.

Hearth & Honey opened with a single deck oven, a secondhand espresso machine, and a stubborn belief that a neighborhood is better with a good bakery in it. We spent the first year figuring out sourdough by feel, one very patient starter, and a lot of trial loaves that never made it to the case.

These days the ovens run longer and the espresso machine is newer, but the goal hasn't changed: bake it properly, keep the room warm, and give people a reason to sit down for a minute.

"We still can't agree on the right way to eat a croissant. It's tearing it in half. Fight us."
